  From Google Calendar (configured via CALENDAR_NAME) pull the upcoming meetings for tomorrow's date (tdate + 1) in IST along with attendee lists. Do not run this cron job if it is Friday or Saturday. Call this list of meetings as "meetings_4_tomorrow";

  Read the calendar event body's description as per the format (this acts as the input) for each meeting as "meeting_4_tomorrow" and store it in the list "meetings_4_tomorrow":
    - Agenda: (Mandatory). It captures the meeting topic to be used further as below. Contains meeting agenda text. Call this field as "meeting_4_tomorrow.AGENDA".
    - Company Information:(Mandatory). Contains company contextual information like overview, size, revenue, location etc. Call this field as "meeting_4_tomorrow.COMPANY_INFORMATION".

    - Company Industry:(Mandatory) Industry Domain of the prospect company. Call this field as "meeting_4_tomorrow.PROSPECT_INDUSTRY".
    - Company Country:(Mandatory) Country of the prospect company. Call this field as "meeting_4_tomorrow.COMPANY_COUNTRY".

    - Attachment (in the email itself) which could be multiple. Call it meeting_4_tomorrow.ALL_ATTACHMENTS.
    - Subject: (Mandatory) The subject line of the email, store it as meeting_4_tomorrow.SUBJECT;
